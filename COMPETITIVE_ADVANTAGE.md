## Key Differentiators

### 1. Disruptive Pricing
- **Competitors**: Per-user licensing ($50-500/user/month)
- **Nexus**: Pay-as-you-grow (scale from $0 → $1000/month at 1000 users)
- **Result**: 90% cost savings for SMBs

### 2. AI-First Architecture
- **Competitors**: AI as expensive add-on
- **Nexus**: AI embedded in every feature (predictions, insights, NLQ)
- **Result**: Employees become 2x more productive

### 3. Speed & Experience
- **Competitors**: Minutes to load, clunky interfaces
- **Nexus**: <500ms load times, modern glassmorphic design
- **Result**: Users choose it over competitors

### 4. Mobile-First
- **Competitors**: Mobile-last afterthoughts
- **Nexus**: Native PWA that works offline, installs on home screen
- **Result**: True anywhere/anytime access

### 5. Real-Time Collaboration
- **Competitors**: Stale data, manual syncing
- **Nexus**: Google Docs-like real-time sync everywhere
- **Result**: Teams move faster

### 6. No Lock-In
- **Competitors**: Proprietary formats, expensive migration
- **Nexus**: Open data, instant CSV/JSON export
- **Result**: Customers stay by choice, not lock-in
